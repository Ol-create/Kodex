# Leddar General FAQ

## What is Leddar?

Leddar is a premium leather production platform. Fashion brands order leather
goods (shoes, bags, wallets, belts, leather wear and more), and Leddar matches
each order with a skilled artisan who makes a sample first and then the full
production run. Leddar's admin team manages pricing, quality review, payments
and delivery between the brand and the artisan.

## Who uses Leddar?

There are three kinds of accounts:

- **Brands** request quotes, approve samples and pay for production in the Brand Portal.
- **Artisans** accept jobs, make samples and production orders, upload progress videos and get paid in the Artisan Dashboard.
- **Admins** (the Leddar team) price orders, assign artisans, review videos and release payments in the Admin Panel.

## Why can't I log in after signing up?

Two checks must pass before you can log in:

1. **Verify your email.** Signing up sends a verification email. Until you click the link, login fails with "Please verify your email address".
2. **Wait for admin approval.** New brand and artisan accounts start as pending. Until the Leddar team approves your account, login fails with "Awaiting admin approval".

If your account was rejected, login shows "Your account has been rejected. Please contact support."

## I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use **Forgot password** on the login page and follow the instructions Leddar emails you to set a new password.

## What do I need to verify my identity (KYC)?

Leddar verifies identity through QoreID:

- **Brands** verify a **NIN** (National Identification Number) and a **CAC / RC number** (business registration). Both are required before ordering.
- **Artisans** complete three steps: **NIN**, **business address** and **bank details**.

Each verification is done once.

## How are payments made on Leddar?

Brands pay through **Paystack** checkout. Leddar holds production money in escrow and releases
it to the artisan in stages as the work progresses. Artisans are paid into the
bank account they verified during onboarding.

## Is VAT charged?

Yes. Production payments include **7.5% VAT**. The VAT amount is shown in the
payment confirmation window before you pay, and on your invoice.

## Which leather products can Leddar make?

Artisans on Leddar specialise in: shoes and boots, slippers and sandals, women's
bags, office and travel bags, wallets and belts, small leather goods, and
leather wear, plus other custom items.
