# Leddar Brand FAQ

## How does ordering work from start to finish?

1. Sign up, verify your email and wait for your account to be approved.
2. Complete identity verification (NIN + CAC) on the **Identity Verification** page.
3. **Place an order**: fill in the quote form and upload reference files.
4. Leddar sets the sample price and notifies you. Review it in **My Quotes** and pay the **sample fee**.
5. An artisan makes the sample. Once Leddar has reviewed it, you get a **sample video** to watch.
6. **Approve** the sample, or **request changes**.
7. After approval, you receive production pricing. Pay the **production balance**.
8. Follow production and delivery in **Track My Order** until it is delivered.

## Why can't I place an order?

You must finish identity verification first. Both your **NIN** and your
**CAC / RC number** must be verified before you can place any order. Until then,
a reminder appears after every login. Go to **Identity Verification** (`/kyc`)
to complete it.

## Why can't I see my dashboard or features after signing up?

Signing up only requests access. You need to verify your email and wait for
Leddar to approve your account before you can log in. After that, ordering
features stay locked until your NIN and CAC verification is complete.

## How much does a sample cost?

The sample fee is a flat fee that Leddar sets for each order after reviewing
your quote request. You'll get a notification when it's ready; see the pricing
in **My Quotes** before paying. Leddar doesn't publish a fixed price list.

## How is the production balance calculated?

Production balance = (materials + labour) × 1.075 (7.5% VAT) − sample credit.

The sample fee you already paid is credited against the production balance, so
you don't pay for the sample twice.

## When do I see the sample video?

Only after Leddar's team has reviewed and approved it. The artisan's upload goes
to Leddar first; you don't see it the moment the artisan uploads it. Once
forwarded, the video appears in **My Quotes** (`/order-status`) and you get a
notification.

## Can I request changes to my sample?

Yes. When you watch the sample video you can **Approve**, **Reject** or
**Request changes**. You can request a **maximum of 2 corrections**. Each time,
the artisan re-uploads a new video. After 2 corrections you can only approve or
reject.

## What happens after I approve the sample?

Leddar marks the sample complete and creates your production order, then sends
production pricing. Once you pay the production balance, the order moves into
production with the same artisan who made your sample.

## How do I pay?

All payments use **Paystack** inline checkout from the Brand Portal. When a
payment succeeds, Paystack notifies Leddar automatically and your order status
updates. You don't need to send proof of payment.

## My payment went through but my order didn't update. What now?

Order status updates when Paystack confirms the payment to Leddar, which is
usually immediate. Refresh the page after a minute. If it still hasn't
updated, contact Leddar support with your payment reference.

## Where can I find my invoices?

On the **Payments & Invoices** page (`/invoices`). Every invoice is a PDF you
can download, named like `leddar-invoice-INV-XXXXXXXX.pdf`.

## How do I track my order?

Use **Track My Order** (`/order-tracker`) for a live timeline. After the
artisan finishes production, the order moves through Pending Delivery,
Dispatched (shipped) and Delivered. Delivered orders and their approved sample
video stay in **Order History**.

## What does each order status mean?

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Your request was received and is waiting for pricing |
| Flat fee paid | Sample fee paid; an artisan is being assigned |
| Sample in progress | The artisan is making your sample |
| Sample completed / approved | The sample is done or you approved it |
| Balance paid / In production | You paid the production balance and production has started |
| Pending delivery | Production is finished and approved |
| Shipped | Your order is on its way |
| Delivered | Your order has arrived |

## How do I update my business profile?

Go to **Profile & Settings** (`/profile`).
