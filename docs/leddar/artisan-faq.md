# Leddar Artisan FAQ

## What do I need to complete before I can accept jobs?

All three verification steps on the **Identity Verification** page:

1. **NIN**: verified instantly through QoreID.
2. **Business address**: this step is complete as soon as you save your address. The physical check by a QoreID agent happens later and doesn't hold you back (see below).
3. **Bank details**: your account number and bank, confirmed through Paystack.

You can be **assigned** a job once your NIN is verified, but you can only
**accept** a job after all three steps are done. The Jobs page banner tells you
exactly which steps are missing.

## Why does my address show as pending or "in progress"?

Address verification is a physical visit. When you accept your first job,
Leddar sends your address to QoreID and an agent visits in person, so it is not
instant. You'll get a notification when the result arrives:

- **Verified**: the agent confirmed your address.
- **Not verified**: the agent visited but couldn't confirm it. Contact support to update your address.
- **Failed**: the check couldn't be submitted for technical reasons. Contact Leddar support.

Yes, you can still accept and work on jobs: once your address is saved, the
address step counts as done, and a pending physical check **never blocks** you.

## How long do I have to accept a job?

Each job has an acceptance deadline set by Leddar. There are separate windows
for sample jobs and production jobs, and the deadline is shown on the job. If
you don't accept or decline before it passes, the job **expires** automatically
and Leddar reassigns the order to another artisan.

## Can I decline a job?

Yes. You can decline a job while it is still **Assigned**, before you accept
it. Leddar is notified and reassigns the order.

## What are the stages of a sample job?

Assigned → In Progress → Admin Review → Sample Approved → Completed.

## What are the stages of a production job?

Assigned → In Progress → Admin Review → Pending Delivery → Dispatched → Delivered.

Production jobs normally go to the same artisan who made the approved sample.

## How do I upload my video?

Open the job in **My Jobs**, then click or drag a video file into the upload
area. Any video format works, up to **100 MB**. After uploading, the job moves to
**Admin Review**.

## What happens if my video is rejected?

You'll see Leddar's feedback on the job and can upload a new video. Brands can
also request changes to a sample (up to 2 corrections); each correction means
re-uploading a video.

## How and when do I get paid?

Production payments are released in two stages:

| Stage | When | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1: Materials | After you accept the production job | Your raw materials share |
| Stage 2: Service fee | After the order is delivered or completed | Your service fee |

Sample jobs pay a flat sample fee. Payments go to your verified bank account.

## Why is my earning different from the current commission rate?

Your earnings use the rates saved when the order was created. If Leddar changes
its commission rates later, orders that already exist aren't affected, so what
you see is exactly what will be released.

## Where can I see my earnings and receipts?

On **My Earnings**: Total Earned (all released payments), Sample Earnings and
Production Earnings, plus your payment history. Receipts download as PDFs named
like `leddar-ORD-XXXXXX.pdf`.

## How do I change my bank account?

Go to **Bank Details** to add or update your account. It's confirmed through
Paystack.
